Save as PDF or print your conversations with any AI in a clean, readable view — no menus, buttons or sidebars.

It used to work only with ChatGPT; since version 2.0 it works with the main AI assistants and with any web page.

WORKS WITH
• The main AI assistants, such as ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini.
• Any other website: prints the main content of the page.
• Selected text: select a part and only that part is printed.

WHAT YOU GET
• Your prompts and the answers, in order and clearly separated.
• Syntax-highlighted code with the language name, whether the site uses Prism, Highlight.js or something else.
• Tables, lists, links, images and math formulas.
• Full conversations, including long ones the site loads in chunks.
• A PDF that is always clean and readable, even if you use dark mode.

SETTINGS BEFORE PRINTING
• Edit the document title.
• Hide your prompts and keep only the answers.
• Show or hide who is speaking in each message.
• Compact mode and three font sizes.
• Dark theme for reading on screen.
• A small footer credit in the PDF, which you can remove with one checkbox.

IN YOUR LANGUAGE
Spanish, English, French, German, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil and Portugal), Dutch, Polish, Turkish, Russian, Japanese, Korean and Chinese (Simplified and Traditional).

HOW TO USE
1. Pin the extension to the toolbar: puzzle-piece icon → pin. A welcome screen explains it after installing.
2. Open the conversation on the AI's website.
3. Click the extension icon (or Alt+Shift+P).
4. Check the clean view, click "Print / Save PDF" and choose "Save as PDF".

USE CASES
• Keep questions and answers for study.
• Print code examples for classes or workshops.
• Export a conversation for technical documentation.
• Share a clean PDF with clients or teammates.

PRIVACY
The extension only reads the tab when you click the icon. Everything is processed in your browser: your conversations are never sent to any server, and there are no analytics or third-party services. It only stores your preferences and a usage counter in your browser, to occasionally invite you to rate it or donate (always optional).
